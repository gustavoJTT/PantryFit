# Glossary

This glossary defines the main terms used in the PantryFit system and its documentation.

## Navigation

- [Documentation index](../README.md)

## A

### API
Application Programming Interface. A set of endpoints used to allow communication between the frontend, backend, and other services.

### Adjustment
A manual change to the stock quantity of a food item, usually used to correct differences, losses, spoilage, or inventory validation.

## C

### Calories
A unit used to measure the energy value of food. In PantryFit, calories are calculated from the nutritional values of the ingredients used in a meal.

### Category
A classification used to organize food items, such as grains, fruits, proteins, dairy, beverages, or condiments.

### Consumption
The act of using a food item as part of a meal or daily intake. Consumption reduces stock quantity and may affect nutritional calculations.

## E

### Entry
A stock movement that increases the quantity of a food item. It usually represents purchases, restocking, or new inventory inputs.

## F

### Food Item
An individual product stored in the pantry, such as rice, chicken, milk, eggs, or oil. It represents a stockable product in the system.

### Food Management
The set of processes related to creating, updating, querying, and maintaining food items and their information.

## I

### Ingredient
A food item used as part of a meal recipe. Each ingredient is associated with a quantity when a meal is registered.

### Inventory
The complete record of available food items and their respective quantities in the pantry. It represents the current stock state of the system.

### Inventory Log
A historical record of stock movements such as entries, withdrawals, and adjustments.

## M

### Macronutrient
A nutrient required in relatively large amounts by the body, mainly proteins, carbohydrates, and fats. PantryFit calculates these values based on meal composition.

### Meal
A recorded food intake event, such as breakfast, lunch, dinner, or snack. Each meal may contain multiple ingredients and quantities.

### Meal History
The chronological record of previously registered meals and their associated items, quantities, and nutritional data.

## N

### Nutritional Information
A set of data describing the caloric and macronutrient values of a meal or food item.

## P

### Pantry
The collection of food items available to the user for daily consumption and inventory control.

### Pantry Item
A product stored in the pantry and managed by the system. It can be tracked by quantity, category, and unit of measure.

### Protein
A macronutrient used for tissue building and repair. It is calculated based on the composition of the ingredients used in a meal.

## Q

### Quantity
The amount of a specific food item available or used. It may be measured in units, grams, milliliters, or another unit configured by the system.

## S

### Stock
The current available amount of a food item in the pantry.

### Stock Level
The quantity currently available for a given item in the inventory. It may be compared to a minimum threshold to trigger alerts or warnings.

### Stock Movement
Any change in stock quantity, such as entry, withdrawal, or adjustment.

### Stock Threshold
A predefined minimum or target quantity used to indicate whether inventory is low or needs attention.

## T

### Transaction
A logical operation that updates inventory or meal data consistently, preserving data integrity.

## U

### Unit of Measure
The measurement system used for a food item, such as grams, milliliters, or units.

## W

### Withdrawal
A stock movement that decreases the quantity of a food item. It commonly occurs when ingredients are consumed in a meal.

---

## Domain-specific terms summary

- Pantry: storage of food items available to the user.
- Meal: defined food consumption record.
- Ingredient: item used to compose a meal.
- Inventory: current state of all stored food items.
- Stock movement: any entry, withdrawal, or adjustment in inventory.
- Nutritional information: calories and macronutrients associated with food intake.

This glossary should be used alongside the system documentation to standardize the terminology adopted in PantryFit.
